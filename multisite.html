Common multisite center tasks that slow you down

Running several centers means dozens of daily jobs, and the time adds up quickly when you work on paper or juggle separate tools. If these sound familiar, see how THINKBIG brings them into one place:

  • Collecting fees from every center by hand
  • Typing enrollment details into spreadsheets
  • Merging attendance reports from each center
  • Copying staff rotas between tools
  • Keeping fee plans in sync across locations
  • Writing invoices for every family by hand
  • Emailing families one by one about updates
  • Chasing consent forms on paper
  • Building group reports from scratch
  • Managing a separate login for every center

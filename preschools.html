Why preschools love THINKBIG

Simplify your preschool’s day with THINKBIG. From fee plans and invoices to attendance and daily diaries, everything your preschool does each day lives in one calm place.

  • Streamlined admin tasks

    Spend less time on registers, meal logs and paperwork, and more time with children.

  • Real-time family communication

    Share the day with parents through photos and updates, without extra calls and texts after hours.

  • On-time fees and payments

    Send invoices automatically, follow up on what is due and make paying easy for every family.

Common preschool tasks that slow you down

Running a preschool involves dozens of small daily jobs, and the time adds up quickly when you work on paper or juggle separate tools. If these sound familiar, see how THINKBIG helps:

  • Calling families one by one about fees
  • Writing attendance on paper registers
  • Chasing permission slips and forms
  • Copying enquiries between notebooks and spreadsheets
  • Texting photos to parents individually
  • Updating waitlists by hand
  • Working out staff rotas on paper
  • Recording meals and naps on clipboards
  • Writing progress reports from scattered notes
  • Answering the same questions in group chats

Common day care tasks that slow you down

Caring for little ones leaves little time for paperwork. THINKBIG helps you take these off your plate:

  • Writing meal and nap logs by hand
  • Phoning families about late pickups
  • Keeping allergy lists on the fridge
  • Collecting fees in cash and tracking them in notebooks
  • Sending photos to each family separately
  • Checking who is allowed to collect a child
  • Juggling staff shifts on paper
  • Filing incident notes in folders
  • Counting children on paper registers
  • Answering “how was her day?” one parent at a time
